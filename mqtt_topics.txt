Voice Controlled Smart Home Automation - MQTT Configuration

MQTT Broker Details
-------------------
Broker Address : broker.hivemq.com
Protocol       : MQTT
Connection     : WiFi

MQTT Topics Used
----------------

1. Bedroom Light Control
Topic: home/bedroom/light
Commands:
ON  -> Turn ON bedroom light
OFF -> Turn OFF bedroom light

2. Hall Light Control
Topic: home/hall/light
Commands:
ON  -> Turn ON hall light
OFF -> Turn OFF hall light

3. DC Fan Control
Topic: home/fan/control
Commands:
ON  -> Turn ON fan
OFF -> Turn OFF fan

Optional Status Topics
----------------------

Bedroom Light Status:
home/bedroom/status

Hall Light Status:
home/hall/status

Fan Status:
home/fan/status
